## 1. Relationship

1. The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a relationship of employment, partnership, agency or joint venture.
2. The Contractor is not entitled to employment benefits, statutory contributions or leave from the Company.
3. The Contractor has no authority to bind the Company or to represent that it may do so.

## 2. Services

1. The Contractor shall provide the services described in Schedule A (the "Services").
2. The Contractor shall determine the manner and method of performing the Services, using its own equipment, subject to meeting the deliverables and dates in Schedule A.
3. The Contractor may engage substitutes only with the Company's prior written consent, and remains responsible for their work and for their compliance with this Agreement.

## 3. Fees and invoicing

1. Fees: **[₹X per [hour / day / month / deliverable]]**, exclusive of GST where applicable.
2. The Contractor shall invoice **[monthly in arrears]**, and the Company shall pay within **[30]** days of receipt of a valid invoice bearing the correct GSTIN, description and SAC code.
3. Tax shall be deducted at source at the applicable statutory rate, and the certificate furnished within **[30]** days of deposit.
4. Pre-approved expenses are reimbursed at actuals against receipts.
5. The Contractor is solely responsible for its own taxes, registrations and statutory filings in respect of the fees.

## 4. Term and termination

1. This Agreement commences on **[Date]** and continues until **[Date]**, unless terminated earlier.
2. Either party may terminate on **[30]** days' written notice.
3. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not cured within **[15]** days of written notice, or on the other's insolvency.
4. On termination, the Contractor shall deliver all work in progress, Company property and Confidential Information, and the Company shall pay for Services properly performed up to the termination date.

## 5. Intellectual property

1. The Contractor assigns to the Company, with effect from receipt of the fees payable in respect of the relevant work, all intellectual property rights in all work product created in the course of providing the Services.
2. The Contractor waives, to the extent permitted by law, all moral rights in the work product.
3. The Contractor shall execute such further documents as the Company reasonably requires to perfect this assignment.
4. The Contractor retains its pre-existing intellectual property, tools and methodologies, and grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use them to the extent embedded in the work product.
5. The Contractor warrants that the work product is original, does not infringe any third-party rights, and that any third-party or open-source components used are disclosed in Schedule B together with their licence terms.

## 6. Confidentiality

The Contractor shall keep confidential all information of the Company and its clients received in connection with the Services, use it only for the Services, and not disclose it to any third party. This obligation survives termination for **[3]** years, and indefinitely in respect of trade secrets.

## 7. Non-solicitation

During the term and for **[12]** months after, the Contractor shall not solicit for employment or engagement any employee of the Company with whom it had material dealings, save in response to a general public advertisement.

## 8. Indemnity and liability

1. The Contractor indemnifies the Company against claims arising from the Contractor's breach of this Agreement, from any claim that the work product infringes third-party intellectual property, and from any claim by any authority arising from the Contractor's own tax or statutory obligations.
2. Save for the matters in clause 8.1, fraud and wilful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability is limited to the fees paid under this Agreement in the **[12]** months preceding the claim, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit.

## 9. General

1. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and may be varied only in writing signed by both.
2. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.
3. Notices shall be given in writing to the addresses above and by email to **[email addresses]**, and are deemed received on the next business day after sending.
4. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.
5. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.

## 10. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[India]**. Disputes shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the **[Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996]**, seat at **[City]**, in English.
