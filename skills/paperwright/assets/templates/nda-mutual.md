## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss **[describe the opportunity narrowly — e.g. a potential engagement of Party A by Party B for [scope]]** (the "Purpose"). Confidential Information may be disclosed only for the Purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

1. "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure, including commercial terms, pricing, customer and supplier information, technical information, source code, business plans and the existence and content of the discussions.
2. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations

1. The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, shall keep it confidential, and shall protect it with at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information and no less than reasonable care.
2. Disclosure is permitted only to those directors, employees, professional advisers and affiliates who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations no less protective than these. The Receiving Party remains responsible for their compliance.
3. No copies beyond what the Purpose requires.

## 4. Compelled disclosure

If required by law, regulation or an order of a court or regulator, the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information, provided it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice where lawful, discloses only what is required, and seeks confidential treatment where available.

## 5. Term and survival

1. This Agreement commences on the date above and continues for **[2]** years.
2. The confidentiality obligations survive for **[3]** years from the date of each disclosure, and indefinitely in respect of any information that constitutes a trade secret for so long as it remains one.

## 6. Return and destruction

On written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy Confidential Information and confirm in writing that it has done so, except for copies retained in routine backups or required by law or regulatory policy, which remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.

## 7. No licence, no warranty, no obligation

1. No licence or other right in any intellectual property is granted by this Agreement.
2. Confidential Information is provided as is, with no warranty as to accuracy or completeness.
3. Nothing obliges either Party to proceed with any transaction or to disclose anything.

## 8. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach, and that a Disclosing Party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy available.

## 9. General

1. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be varied only in writing signed by both Parties.
2. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.
3. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.
4. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, and scanned or electronic copies have the same effect as originals.
5. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor of all or substantially all of its business.

Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the **[Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996]**, seat at **[City]**, proceedings in English, award final and binding. Nothing prevents either Party from seeking interim relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.
